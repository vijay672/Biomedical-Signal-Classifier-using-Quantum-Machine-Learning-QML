# QFF 2026 Phase 1 PS-Q1 — judge summary

**Team:** MOM'S MAGIC — MATTA VIJAY, GUDE YUVA NIKESH, BOLLA DINESH KUMAR. **Scope:** one four-qubit RY-encoded, two-layer ring VQC and a logistic baseline on a 200-row, four-feature synthetic binary fixture; Aer comparison of participant-designed Processor A (5-qubit line) and B (9-qubit grid). The latest Master Guide governs the Phase 1 topology freedom and A/B sizes.

**Dataset provenance caveat:** the Master Guide specifies three informative features but its literal `make_classification` call fails with scikit-learn defaults. We explicitly set `n_redundant=0` as an **unconfirmed engineering assumption** and hash the fixture, split and train-fitted scaler. The 160/40 split is stratified with seed 42. Synthetic label 1 is not a clinically validated abnormal ECG diagnosis.

These are [saved predictions](../results/tables/q1_provisional_processor_benchmark.json) checked against [fresh metric recalculation and exact QNN score replay](../results/tables/q1_judge_audit.json). The classical baseline is stronger. The official VQC predicts only 10/40 rows positive; its score distributions overlap around 0.5. [New circuit tests](../tests/test_circuit_semantics.py) verify exact gate order, all 16 parity outcomes and A/B measurement mapping, and show that `w[9]`, `w[10]`, `w[11]`, `w[13]`, `w[14]`, and `w[15]` do not affect measured class probabilities for this readout (the four final-layer RZ angles and final-layer RY on q1/q3). A **separate** 128-fit/32-validation experiment chose threshold 0.45 on validation only (precision ≥0.55, F1 ≥0.60), then achieved held-out recall 0.800, precision 0.516, F1 0.627, accuracy 0.525 and specificity 0.250. It is [separately labeled](../results/tables/q1_recall_calibration.json) and does not replace the official weights or A/B comparison.

Both processors use the identical frozen model SHA256 `9902dd5dce0d069633081b7787618f98797fc1442c5ceff5ac3679067c8332fd`, test rows, `rz/sx/x/cx`, level-1 SABRE layout/routing, seed 42, 4096 shots and synthetic depolarizing/readout probabilities 0.001/0.005/0.01. The unconstrained native reference has 8 CX, gate depth 20. Official A compiles to 24 CX/depth 35 (+16 CX/+15 depth, six explicit routing-stage SWAPs); B to 8 CX/depth 20 (zero overhead/SWAPs). A controlled **transpilation-only** initial-layout ablation gives A 22 CX/depth 34 and B 14 CX/depth 26 under trivial layout. A [three-seed compiler check](../results/tables/q1_seed_robustness.json) retained 24 versus 8 CX, though A's depth varied 33–35. B's cost advantage is specific to this circuit and tested settings. The noisy A/B classification difference has only one A-only versus three B-only correct examples (exploratory paired p=0.625); see [uncertainty review](../results/tables/q1_statistical_review.json). No universal processor or inference superiority is claimed.
