## Circuit, compiler and provenance

The official [Qiskit builder](../src/vqc.py) has four RY input gates, two layers of four RY/four RZ rotations plus directed `CX(0,1),(1,2),(2,3),(3,0)`, 16 distinct weights, eight logical CX and measurement of all four qubits. `SamplerQNN` groups all 16 outcomes by odd parity. [Exact structure and 16-outcome tests](../tests/test_circuit_semantics.py) also verify that routing preserves the full ideal outcome distribution after using the recorded classical-to-physical measurement mapping. Saved logical and transpiled circuit [text](../results/tables/q1_provisional_logical_circuit.txt) and [Qiskit-generated SVGs](../results/figures/logical_circuit.svg) match deterministic compilation. The D topology diagram is a schematic, not evidence of exact transpilation.

The [frozen official model](../results/tables/q1_provisional_vqc_model.json) has SHA256 `9902dd5dce0d069633081b7787618f98797fc1442c5ceff5ac3679067c8332fd`, used identically by A and B. The [benchmark JSON](../results/tables/q1_provisional_processor_benchmark.json) records dataset/model identities, basis `rz/sx/x/cx`, level 1, SABRE layout/routing, seed 42, 4096 shots and shared synthetic noise probabilities 0.001 single-qubit depolarizing, 0.005 CX depolarizing and 0.01 symmetric readout flip. The unconstrained reference costs eight CX/gate depth 20. A is +16 CX/+15 gate depth; B has zero overhead. Routing-stage six/zero SWAP nodes are counted *before native-basis decomposition*, not inferred from CX deltas. Four of A's five and four of B's nine physical qubits carry logical data at the recorded placement. The [seed-7/42/99 check](../results/tables/q1_seed_robustness.json) is compilation-only and does not replace seed 42.
